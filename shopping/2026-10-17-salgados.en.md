# Shopping list — Brazilian snacks (weekend of 2026-10-17)

For: [pastel](../recipes/pastel-de-feira/en.md), [coxinha & cheese balls](../recipes/coxinha-e-bolinha-de-queijo/en.md), [empadinha](../recipes/empadinha-de-frango/en.md).

## Meat
- [ ] 900 g chicken breast (Hähnchenbrustfilet)
- [ ] 500 g ground beef (Rinderhackfleisch)

## Dairy
- [ ] 300 g shredded mozzarella (Mozzarella gerieben) — pastel
- [ ] 350 g block mozzarella (Pizza-Mozzarella am Stück) — cheese balls
- [ ] 200 g full-fat cream cheese (Frischkäse Doppelrahmstufe)
- [ ] 1 block butter (250 g)
- [ ] 1 tub lard (plain Schweineschmalz)
- [ ] 1 L milk
- [ ] 6 eggs

## Produce
- [ ] 4 floury potatoes (mehligkochend)
- [ ] 3 onions
- [ ] 1 head of garlic
- [ ] 1 tomato
- [ ] parsley and chives (Petersilie, Schnittlauch)

## Pantry
- [ ] 2 kg flour, Type 405 (optional: +1 kg Type 550 for the pastel)
- [ ] breadcrumbs (Paniermehl), ≥ 300 g
- [ ] 2–3 L sunflower or rapeseed oil
- [ ] 1 jar pitted green olives
- [ ] sweet paprika (Paprika edelsüß)
- [ ] chicken stock cubes (Hühnerbrühe)
- [ ] bay leaves (Lorbeerblätter)

## Equipment
- [ ] mini pie tins (Tartelette-Förmchen) or a muffin tray (Muffinblech)
- [ ] kitchen thermometer (optional)

## Check at home
- [x] cachaça (Velho Barreiro)
- [ ] salt, black pepper, olive oil

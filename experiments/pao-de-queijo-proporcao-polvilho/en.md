# Experiment: sour × sweet cassava starch ratio

**Question:** which ratio of sour (*azedo*) to sweet (*doce*) cassava starch makes the best pão de queijo?

**Variable:** % sour starch in the total starch (100 g per batch).
**Everything else held constant:** same liquids, same egg mix, same cheese, same ball weight, same bake.

## Batches

| Batch | Sour | Sweet |
|---|---|---|
| A100 | 100 g | 0 g |
| A75 | 75 g | 25 g |
| A50 | 50 g | 50 g |
| A25 | 25 g | 75 g |
| A0 | 0 g | 100 g |

## Per-batch amounts (recipe ÷ 4)

| Ingredient | Per batch | Total (5 batches) |
|---|---|---|
| Starch (sour + sweet) | 100 g | 250 g sour + 250 g sweet |
| Salted liquid (see below) | 75 g | 375 g |
| Oil | 25 g | 125 g |
| Beaten egg | 37.5 g | 187.5 g (~4 large eggs) |
| Grated cheese (pre-mixed) | 87.5 g | 437.5 g (~300 g Gouda + 140 g Parmesan) |

**Salted liquid:** dissolve **6.25 g salt** in **250 ml milk + 125 ml water**. Each batch then gets 1.25 g salt without a precision scale.

## Protocol

1. **Mise en place:** weigh each batch's starch into 5 labelled bowls. Beat all the eggs together. Grate and mix both cheeses in one bowl.
2. **Scald one batch at a time:** in a small pot weigh 75 g salted liquid + 25 g oil, bring to a boil and pour **immediately** over the starch. Same procedure for every batch so evaporation is equal.
3. Let all batches cool to warm (~15 min).
4. **Egg:** 37.5 g into each batch, all of it, **even if consistency differs**. Dough texture differences are data: sour and sweet starch absorb differently. Note how each looked.
5. **Cheese:** 87.5 g into each batch.
6. **30 g balls** (weigh them!). Each batch makes ~10. Bake **4 of each**, freeze the rest.
7. **Single bake**, 180 °C, 25–35 min. Ovens have hot spots: spread each batch's balls across different tray positions, or rotate the tray halfway. Identify them with a tray map (photo) or toothpicks.
8. Evaluate **warm, ~10 min** out of the oven.

## Measurements

- **Rise:** diameter and height (ruler), mean of 4.
- **Crust:** crispness 1–5.
- **Crumb:** chewiness/stretch 1–5; hollow? (cut in half)
- **Flavor:** sourness 1–5; overall 1–5.
- **Blind** if possible: someone else serves without saying which batch is which.

# Experiment 2: cheese amount

**Context:** in [experiment 1](../pao-de-queijo-proporcao-polvilho/en.md) the 75 % sour / 25 % sweet batch was preferred. But no batch rose much, and the bread came out cheesy and not very airy. Since size didn't change with the starch ratio, the limiting factor is something else.

**Question:** does less cheese make the bread taller and airier without losing flavor?

**Variable:** grams of cheese per 100 g starch.

**Constant:** 75 % sour / 25 % sweet, liquids, egg, 30 g balls, same bake.

**Process change from experiment 1, applied to every batch:** oven at 210 °C for the first 12 min, then 180 °C. Batch Q87 repeats experiment 1's cheese level, so comparing it with experiment 1 shows the effect of the oven. That comparison is only indicative, since it was made on another day.

## Batches

| Batch | Cheese per 100 g starch |
|---|---|
| Q50 | 50 g |
| Q70 | 70 g |
| Q87 (control) | 87.5 g |

## Amounts

| Ingredient | Per batch | Total (3 batches) |
|---|---|---|
| Sour starch | 75 g | 225 g |
| Sweet starch | 25 g | 75 g |
| Salted liquid | 75 g | 225 g (150 ml milk + 75 ml water + 3.75 g salt) |
| Oil | 25 g | 75 g |
| Beaten egg | 37.5 g | 112.5 g (beat 3 eggs and weigh) |
| Cheese (Gouda + Parmesan, pre-mixed) | 50 / 70 / 87.5 g | 207.5 g (~150 g Gouda + 60 g Parmesan) |

Salt is held constant to isolate the cheese. Since Parmesan also adds salt, the low-cheese batches may taste less salty. Note it.

## Protocol

1. **Mise en place:** weigh each batch's starch into a labelled bowl. Beat the eggs together. Grate and mix the cheeses.
2. **Scald one batch at a time:** in a small pot heat 75 g salted liquid + 25 g oil to a **rolling boil** (big bubbles) and pour it **immediately**. Mix until no dry starch is left.
3. Let it cool to warm (~15 min).
4. **Egg:** 37.5 g per batch. **Beat vigorously for 3–5 min** (spoon or mixer) until smooth and stretchy.
5. **Cheese:** the batch's amount.
6. **30 g balls, weighed.** Bake 4 per batch, spread across the tray. Take a photo to record the positions.
7. **Oven preheated to 210 °C** for at least 15 min. Bake 12 min **without opening**, then lower to 180 °C until golden (+10–15 min).
8. Evaluate warm, ~10 min out of the oven.

## Measurements

With a ruler this time: the expected difference may be too small to judge by eye.

- **Height and diameter** of each of the 4 balls. Record the mean.
- **Crumb:** airy/hollow 1–5, chewy 1–5.
- **Cheese flavor** 1–5; **salt** (low / ok / high).
- **Overall** 1–5, blind if possible.

**Expectation:** less cheese → taller and airier, less cheese flavor. The likely sweet spot is Q70.

## If nothing changes

If all three batches rise little and look alike, cheese isn't the limit. The next suspects are the **sour starch** (try another brand, ideally sun-dried) and the **scalding** (make one larger batch so the liquid doesn't cool).

# Chicken empadinha

Brazilian mini chicken pies: a melt-in-the-mouth shortcrust (more lard than butter) with a creamy filling.

## Ingredients

### Pastry
- 500 g wheat flour, German Type 405
- 1 tsp salt
- 150 g lard (plain Schweineschmalz — no Grieben, onion or apple), cold
- 75 g cold butter, cubed
- 1 egg
- 3–4 tbsp ice water

### Filling
- 400 g chicken breast (cooked and shredded)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, chopped
- 1 tomato, seeded and chopped
- 1 tbsp flour
- 200 ml chicken cooking broth
- 100 g cream cheese (Frischkäse)
- 50 g green olives, chopped
- 2 tbsp chopped parsley & chives
- ½ tsp salt

### Egg wash
- 1 egg yolk + 1 tsp milk

## Method

1. **Pastry:** mix flour and salt. Rub in the lard and butter with your fingertips until it looks like coarse crumbs. Add the egg and the water gradually, **just until it comes together. Don't knead.** Wrap and chill 30 min.
2. **Filling:** sauté onion, garlic and tomato in the oil, add the chicken, sprinkle the flour and pour in the broth. Cook until thick and creamy (~10 min). Off the heat, stir in cream cheese, olives, herbs and salt. **Cool completely.**
3. **Line:** oven to 200 °C. Press ~30 g balls into the base and sides of each tin (3–4 mm thick). Grease a muffin tray if using one.
4. **Fill and cap:** don't overfill; top with a thin disc and seal the edges well.
5. **Brush** with the egg wash.
6. **Bake:** 200 °C, 25–30 min, until golden.
7. **Unmold:** let cool well (≥ 15 min). Lard-heavy pastry is fragile when warm.

## Why (fat choice)

| Fat | Texture | Flavor/color |
|---|---|---|
| 250 g butter | firmer | most flavor, browns more |
| 175 g butter + 75 g lard | crumbly | balanced |
| **150 g lard + 75 g butter** (chosen) | almost as short as all-lard | some butter flavor and color |
| ~200 g lard only | shortest, most fragile | neutral, browns less |

Lard is ~100 % fat; butter ~82 % (the rest is water). That's why the total weight drops when lard goes in, and the ice water is adjusted by feel.

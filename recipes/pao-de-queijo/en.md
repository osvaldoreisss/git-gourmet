# Brazilian cheese bread (pão de queijo mineiro)

Scalded cassava starch, aged cheese, oven. The sour/sweet starch ratio is being tested in [`experiments/`](../../experiments/pao-de-queijo-proporcao-polvilho/en.md).

## Ingredients

- 400 g sour cassava starch (*polvilho azedo*; Brazilian/Latin shops or online: Yoki, Amafil)
- 200 ml milk
- 100 ml water
- 100 g vegetable oil
- 5 g salt
- 150 g eggs (~3 large)
- 350 g grated *queijo meia cura* (semi-aged Brazilian cheese)
  - in Germany: **250 g medium-aged Gouda (mittelalt) + 100 g Parmesan/Grana Padano**

> **Sweet** starch (*polvilho doce*) = the *Tapiokastärke* sold in Asian shops. **Sour** starch is fermented and sun-dried: it rises more and bakes crispier and hollower.

## Method

1. Bring milk, water, oil and salt to a boil.
2. Pour it boiling over the starch and mix, pressing out lumps, until all of it is scalded. It will look crumbly and sticky — that's normal.
3. Let it cool to just **warm** (~15 min), or the egg will cook.
4. Add the eggs gradually, kneading, until the dough is soft and slightly sticky. If it gets too loose, hold back the last egg.
5. Mix in the cheese until even.
6. Oven at 180 °C. With oiled hands, roll 30–35 g balls and space them out.
7. Bake 25–35 min, until golden and firm. **Don't open the oven in the first 15 min.** _(Time/temperature filled in — the post was cut off.)_

Freeze raw and bake straight from frozen: 180 °C, 35–40 min.

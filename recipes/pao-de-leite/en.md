# Cold-fermented milk bread rolls

Soft, slightly sweet enriched dough made on Friday night: half baked Saturday (~12 h in the fridge), half on Sunday (~36 h).

## Ingredients

- 500 g wheat flour, German Type 550 (or 405)
- 3 g instant dry yeast (*Trockenhefe*) — **weigh it**, it's less than half a packet
- 50 g sugar
- 8 g salt
- 240 ml milk at room temperature (not warm)
- 3 eggs
- 40 g softened butter
- up to 50 g extra flour, only if the dough is far too slack
- milk for brushing (or yolk + milk for more shine and color)

> **Same-day version** (no fridge): 7 g yeast and warm milk (~35 °C); first rise 60–90 min, second 40–60 min.

## Method

### Friday night
1. Mix flour, yeast and sugar; add the salt. Whisk the eggs with the milk and pour over the dry ingredients.
2. In a stand mixer **with the dough hook**: 5 min, add the butter gradually, then 8–10 min more, until the dough pulls away from the sides and passes the windowpane test.
   - At first the dough looks almost like batter. Keep mixing before adding flour; if it stays that way, add flour 25 g at a time until it clears the sides.
3. Weigh and split into two ~500 g halves.
4. **Saturday half:** rest 30 min, shape 6 balls of ~80 g on a parchment-lined tray, cover with oiled cling film, fridge (back shelf, ~4 °C).
5. **Sunday half:** whole, in an oiled lidded container (room to grow 2–3×), fridge.

### Saturday
6. Morning: take the tray out 60–90 min before baking; rolls should grow ~1.5× and a fingertip dent should spring back slowly. Preheat to 180 °C for the last 30 min.
7. Brush and bake at 180 °C for 18–22 min, until golden.
8. Punch down the Sunday dough in its container.
9. Evening (~8 pm): shape the 6 Sunday rolls from the cold dough, tray, cover, fridge.

### Sunday
10. Same as Saturday: 45–90 min out of the fridge, brush, 180 °C, 18–22 min.

## Why

- **Reduced yeast (3 g, 0.6 % of flour):** the fridge slows yeast down but doesn't stop it. With 7 g, the 36-h half would overproof.
- **Room-temperature milk:** the dough goes into the fridge cooler and the yeast starts slower.
- **Salt at 1.6 % of flour:** the original "pinch" gave bland bread and runaway fermentation.
- **Butter instead of margarine, 40 g:** more flavor and softness.
- **~500 g flour:** calculated for ~60–65 % hydration (milk + egg water ≈ 320 g).

## Test notes

### Test 1 — 2026-11-06 to 08
- Milk: 250 ml by mistake (+10 ml); extra flour: 3 tbsp (~25–30 g) because the dough was very slack in the mixer.
- Brushed with **milk only**.
- Result: **wonderful** both days.
- Preference: the **~36 h** rolls seemed even better than the ~12 h ones. Possible bias: not blind, and eaten on different days. Next time: bake both on the same day (e.g. doughs made 24 h apart) and taste blind.

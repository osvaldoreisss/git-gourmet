# Brazilian street-market pastel (beef & cheese)

Thin, crispy, blistered fried pastry. The cachaça in the dough creates the bubbles.

## Ingredients

### Dough
- 500 g wheat flour (German Type 550 or 405)
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 3 tbsp cachaça (Velho Barreiro)
- ~215 ml warm water (add gradually)

### Filling
- 500 g ground beef (Rinderhackfleisch)
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, chopped
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp sweet paprika (Paprika edelsüß)
- 3 tbsp chopped parsley & chives
- 300 g shredded mozzarella (Mozzarella gerieben)

### Frying
- ~2 L sunflower or rapeseed oil

## Method

1. **Dough:** mix flour and salt. Make a well, add oil and cachaça, then add water little by little until the dough no longer sticks to your hands. It should be firm.
2. **Knead:** ~10 min, until smooth and elastic.
3. **Rest:** wrap in cling film, 30 min at room temperature or 1 h in the fridge.
4. **Filling:** sauté onion and garlic in a little oil, add the beef and cook on high heat until dry and browned. Season with salt, pepper and paprika, turn off the heat and stir in the herbs. **Cool completely.**
5. **Roll:** divide the dough into 4 and roll very thin (1–2 mm). Cut ~12 × 20 cm rectangles.
6. **Fill:** 1 tbsp beef + some cheese on one half. Brush the edge with water, fold, press out the air and seal with a fork.
7. **Fry:** oil at 185 °C in a deep pot (≥ 8 cm of oil). 1–2 at a time, ~2 min, spooning oil over the top. Drain on a rack.

## Why

- **Cachaça:** the alcohol evaporates instantly in hot oil and forms the bubbles.
- **No vinegar:** the original used 2 tbsp cachaça + 1 tbsp apple vinegar. We swapped the vinegar for +1 tbsp cachaça, which also relaxes the gluten. Don't go beyond ~4 tbsp: the dough gets crumbly and tears.
- Raw dough keeps 2 days in the fridge, well wrapped.

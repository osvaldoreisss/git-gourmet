# Coxinha & cheese balls with potato dough

One dough, split in half: half becomes chicken coxinhas (teardrop-shaped croquettes), half becomes cheese balls.

## Ingredients

### Dough (full batch)
- 700 g wheat flour, German Type 405, sifted
- 500 g mashed potato (4 medium floury potatoes, *mehligkochend*)
- 500 ml milk
- 500 ml chicken cooking broth
- 2 tbsp margarine or butter
- 2 chicken stock cubes
- salt to taste (~1 tsp)

### Chicken filling (half batch, for the coxinhas)
- 500 g chicken breast (cooked and shredded)
- ½ onion, chopped
- 1 garlic clove, chopped
- ½ tsp sweet paprika (stands in for Brazilian colorau/annatto)
- salt to taste
- ~100 g requeijão or full-fat cream cheese (Frischkäse Doppelrahmstufe; loosen with 1 tbsp milk if stiff)

### Cheese-ball filling
- 350 g firm block mozzarella (Pizza-Mozzarella am Stück) or young Gouda, in 1.5 cm cubes
- Don't use fresh mozzarella in brine: it releases water and the balls burst.

### Breading & frying
- 250 ml milk
- 300 g breadcrumbs (Paniermehl)
- ~2 L sunflower or rapeseed oil

## Method

1. **Chicken:** simmer in water with bay leaves (25 min, or 15 min in a pressure cooker). Keep the broth, shred the meat. _If making the empadinhas the same day, cook all 900 g of chicken together in 1.2 L of water._
2. **Potatoes:** boil peeled until very soft and mash while hot, lump-free.
3. **Chicken filling:** sauté onion and garlic, add chicken and paprika, salt to taste. Turn off the heat and stir in the cream cheese. Add a splash of broth for a juicier filling. **Cool completely.**
4. **Dough:** in a large pot bring broth, milk, margarine, stock cubes, salt and mash to a boil, stirring. Dump in all the flour at once and stir hard until the dough pulls away from the pot (~5 min).
5. **Knead:** on an oiled surface while still warm, until smooth. Cover with cling film. **Split in half.**
6. **Coxinhas:** 60 g dough + ~30 g filling, teardrop shape.
7. **Cheese balls:** 30 g dough + 1 cheese cube. Roll between your palms until no seam is left.
8. **Bread:** milk → breadcrumbs.
9. **(Recommended for the cheese balls)** 20–30 min in the freezer before frying.
10. **Fry:**
    - coxinhas: 175 °C until golden (~4 min);
    - cheese balls: 180 °C, 2–3 min — pull them as soon as they're golden or they burst.

## Tips

- Potato dough is soft; if it sticks, oil your hands instead of adding flour.
- Freezes well raw and breaded. Thaw coxinhas fully before frying.
